- **Know the law that applies.** Some U.S. states, including California, Florida, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania, and Washington, and some other countries generally require the consent of **everyone** in a conversation before it is recorded. Workplace, privacy, and data-protection laws, meeting-platform rules, and your employer's policies may also apply.
- **Tell people and get their consent.** Unless you have confirmed that the law does not require it, tell every participant before you start that the conversation will be recorded, and, if you will use an online service, that it may be sent to that service. Do not use SonoJot to record covertly or where recording is prohibited.
