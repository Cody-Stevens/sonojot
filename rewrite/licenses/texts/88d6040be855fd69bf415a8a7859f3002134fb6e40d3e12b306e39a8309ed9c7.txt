Modifications made to the original work:

  - Exported the PyTorch checkpoint to ONNX (conv_frontend, encoder, decoder graphs) using the
    pipeline at https://github.com/Wasser1462/Qwen3-ASR-onnx.
  - Converted the encoder and decoder graphs from fp32 to fp16 using
    onnxruntime.transformers.optimizer (model_type="qwen3") to fuse RMSNorm/attention ops,
    followed by OnnxModel.convert_float_to_float16(keep_io_types=True).
  - Packaged the resulting files for sherpa-onnx (OfflineRecognizer.from_qwen3_asr).
  - The tokenizer directory is copied unchanged from the base checkpoint.

No fine-tuning or weight modification was performed other than the fp32->fp16 cast.
